Nothing normalises what you return, so its scale sets the policy update size directly and interacts with the trainer's own schedule. Fit the value baseline on a replay of recent iterations rather than the current batch alone, give it real width with several epochs and a long first-iteration warm-up, and normalise the value targets. Add a small annealed exploration term to raise the fraction of seeds that escape the low-return gait - the score is a mean over hidden seeds, so escape rate matters more than a good seed's ceiling.
