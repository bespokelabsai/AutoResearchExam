Do not train a general learner. Classify the family from the observation's empirical rank, identify the latent system by regression from a short probing rollout - including actuator sign, scale and any action delay - and apply the matching analytic controller: linear-quadratic for the near-linear families, energy shaping for swing-up, inverse kinematics for reaching, partial feedback linearisation for cart-pole. Use only quantities invariant to the unknown observation transform. Report a converged policy before the first checkpoint and freeze it, with a validation gate falling back to a learner.
