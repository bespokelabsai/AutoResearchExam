Use the unbiased within-bin U-statistic form of the squared error, with variance from a Hajek projection plus the second-order term, and bin more coarsely than the textbook rate. Then make the critical values asymmetric and per-regime: tightening the lower endpoint costs less coverage than the upper, and slack differs sharply by rank, class count and sample size. Measure coverage against the gate directly, note the systematic dev-to-sealed offset, and stop just above the threshold rather than sweeping blind.
