Serve the whole workload in one batched decode loop over a shared KV cache, grouping prefills by prompt length and swapping finished rows to the end so live rows stay contiguous. Construction and warm-up are untimed, so pre-allocate and pre-touch the KV pool there; then chunk the vocabulary projection with a running max and pre-pack the decode-only weights. Do not try low precision: the token-agreement gate is checked position by position and one divergent argmax corrupts the rest of that request.
