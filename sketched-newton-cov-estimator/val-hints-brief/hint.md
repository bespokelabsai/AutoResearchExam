The limiting covariance solves a Lyapunov recurrence whose sketch moments have closed form; derive it and evaluate exactly rather than replaying the optimizer. Estimate the data covariance by maximum likelihood under its parametric structure and the noise scale from an exact streaming least-squares residual - the noise scale, not the correlation, is what limits accuracy. Accuracy also rises with records consumed, so profile under the same concurrency the grader uses and set the internal deadline so a full pass completes there.
