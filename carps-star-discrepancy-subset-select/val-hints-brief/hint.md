Build an exact evaluator on the grid of the points' own coordinates, then initialise from a cheap closed-form L2 descent - recomputing only the swapped point's kernel column - and refine against the current critical boxes. Spend the budget on many restarts with a patience rule rather than one deep search; weighted variants of the L2 objective give complementary basins. Decide deliberately how to treat points on a boundary coordinate: blanket filtering them looks safe and hurts on skewed clouds.
